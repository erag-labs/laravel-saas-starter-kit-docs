# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want the SaaS Laravel community to be welcoming, respectful and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to Laravel, Vue, React or Svelte.
- Respect different opinions and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and the people using it.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.
- Trolling, spam, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their permission.
- Sharing licence keys, private repository contents or purchased source code in public issues.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to this repository — issues, pull requests, discussions and comments — and to anyone representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, email **erag.office@gmail.com**. You can also use GitHub's **Report content** option on any comment.

All reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate, including:

1. **A private warning** explaining why the behaviour was inappropriate.
2. **A temporary ban** from interacting with the project.
3. **A permanent ban** for serious or repeated violations.

Comments, commits, issues and other contributions that break this Code of Conduct may be edited or removed.
