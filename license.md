## At a glance

| ✅ You may | ❌ You may not |
| --- | --- |
| Use the kit in unlimited projects | Resell, share or give away the kit |
| Build SaaS products, apps and websites for yourself or clients | Publish the source code anywhere public |

| Modify the code as much as you like | Share your repository access with anyone else |
| Keep your code in private repositories | Remove the license or copyright notices |

::: tip Building for a client?
You can deliver a finished application to a client, including the parts of the kit it needs to run. The client may not reuse or resell the kit on its own.
:::

## 1. Who may use the Software

You may use the Software only if you bought it through an official SaaS Laravel purchase, such as a GitHub Sponsors tier listed on the [pricing page](/pricing). "You" means the person or the organization that made the purchase. Members of your team may use the Software only to work on your own End Products.

The "Software" means the SaaS Laravel starter kits (Vue, React and Svelte), including their source code, documentation, designs and updates. The Software is licensed, not sold.

## 2. What you may do

1. Use and modify the Software to build web applications, SaaS products and websites ("End Products").
2. Build unlimited End Products for yourself or for your clients, and use them commercially.
3. Deliver an End Product to a client, including the parts of the Software that are needed to run it.
4. Keep your code, including the Software, in private repositories.
5. Receive updates for as long as you have access to the kit repository.
