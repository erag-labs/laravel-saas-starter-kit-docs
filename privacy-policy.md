# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 27 September 2026

This policy explains what information SaaS Laravel ("we", "us") collects when you visit [saas-laravel.com](https://saas-laravel.com) or buy a starter kit, and how we use it. SaaS Laravel is run by [ERAG](https://erag.in).

## At a glance

| Topic | Summary |
| --- | --- |
| Tracking and analytics | None. No analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. |
| Cookies | None. The site does not set cookies. |
| Payments | Handled entirely by GitHub Sponsors. We never see your card details. |
| What we receive | Your GitHub username and sponsorship details, to give you repository access. |
| Selling data | We never sell or rent your personal information. |

## 1. Visiting the website

The website is a static site hosted on **GitHub Pages**. We do not run analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and the site does not set any cookies. Fonts and images are served from our own domain.

GitHub, as the hosting provider, may log technical information such as your IP address when you request a page, for security and to operate the service. This is covered by the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

### Information stored in your browser

The site saves a few preferences in your browser's local storage. They never leave your device and are never sent to us.

| Key | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `vitepress-theme-appearance` | Remembers light or dark mode |
| `vitepress:local-search-filter` | Remembers your last documentation search |
| `vitepress:local-search-detailed-list` | Remembers the search results layout |

You can clear them at any time in your browser settings.

## 2. Buying a starter kit

Purchases are made through **GitHub Sponsors**. GitHub and its payment partners process your payment, and your payment details are never shared with us. See the [GitHub Sponsors additional terms](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/github-terms/github-sponsors-additional-terms) and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

When you sponsor a tier, GitHub shares the following with us:

- your GitHub username and public profile,
- the tier you chose, the amount and the date,
- your email address, only if you chose to share it with sponsored accounts.

We use this information only to:

- invite your GitHub account to the starter kit repository you bought,
- manage your license and repository access,
- answer your questions and provide support,
- keep records required for accounting and tax purposes.

## 3. Contacting us and GitHub activity

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

Issues, pull requests and comments you post on GitHub are public and are handled by GitHub under its own terms. Please do not post private information in them.

## 4. The starter kits

The starter kits run on your own servers. They do not send any usage data, telemetry or personal information to us. You are responsible for the privacy of the users of the applications you build with the kits.

## 5. Sharing your information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. We share it only:

- with GitHub, as needed to give you repository access,
- when required by law, or to protect our rights and the security of our services.

## 6. How long we keep information

We keep sponsorship and license records for as long as you have access to a kit, and afterwards only as long as needed for legal, accounting or tax reasons. Emails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

## 7. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by emailing [erag.office@gmail.com](mailto:erag.office@gmail.com). Removing your repository access ends your ability to receive updates. Information held by GitHub can be managed in your GitHub account settings.

## 8. Security

We keep the information we receive to a minimum and limit who can access it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

## 9. Children

The website and starter kits are intended for developers and businesses. They are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 16.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.
