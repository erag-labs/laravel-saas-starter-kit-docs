FAQ

Payment questions

Is this a subscription?

No. There is no recurring subscription and nothing renews. You pay once.

Is it a one-time payment?

Yes. Vue $29, React $30 or Svelte $33 for a single kit, or $79 for all three kits, paid once through GitHub Sponsors.

Do I get lifetime access?

Yes. Once you have been invited, you keep access to the kit repository for life.

Are updates included?

Yes. Every update pushed to the kit repository is included at no extra cost.

How often are updates released?

Weekly. Updates are pushed to the kit repositories and you pull them into your project when you are ready.

How do I get repository access?

Automatically. As soon as your GitHub Sponsors payment goes through, your GitHub account is invited to the kit repository. Accept the invitation from GitHub or your email.

Can I buy all three kits?

Yes. The All Starter Kits bundle includes Vue, React and Svelte for $79 — $13 less than buying them separately.

Where do I get support?

On GitHub. Open an issue on the kit repository you have access to.
